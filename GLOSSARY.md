# Language Lab

A personal platform for reaching conversational fluency in several languages, one after another, using only learning methods shown to work in research.

## Language

**Target Language**:
One of the languages being learned: Korean, Spanish, Mandarin, Japanese, Polish.
_Avoid_: Course, track, L2

**Active Language**:
The single Target Language currently receiving new study. There is exactly one at a time.
_Avoid_: Current course, focus language

**Maintained Language**:
A Target Language that is no longer Active but still gets short review so it doesn't fade.
_Avoid_: Finished language, archived language

**Fluency Goal**:
CEFR B1 in speaking and listening for a Target Language. Reading and writing are learned only as far as they support speaking.
_Avoid_: Fluent, proficiency, done

**Mission**:
A concrete real-world situation, spoken or listening only, that the learner wants to handle in a Target Language. Examples: chatting with the shop owner at a Korean market, following a Korean cooking video without subtitles.
_Avoid_: Goal, scenario, quest

**Checkpoint**:
A periodic speaking and listening test that scores the learner against the CEFR "can-do" descriptors, to track progress toward the Fluency Goal.
_Avoid_: Exam, test, assessment

## Study

**Review**:
Spaced recall of sentences already met. The learner recalls aloud, reveals the answer and grades themselves. It happens on the phone, anywhere.
_Avoid_: Flashcards, SRS, drill

**Listening Card**:
A Review item where the learner hears a Target Language sentence and recalls its meaning.
_Avoid_: Recognition card, forward card

**Speaking Card**:
A Review item where the learner sees the English meaning and says the Target Language sentence aloud. It unlocks only after its Listening Card has been passed a few times.
_Avoid_: Production card, reverse card

**Grammar Note**:
A short explanation of a grammar pattern (two or three sentences and an example), shown the first time the pattern appears.
_Avoid_: Grammar lesson, chapter

**History Log**:
The add-only record of every review and session the learner has done, from which all progress and scheduling are calculated.
_Avoid_: Progress, save data, stats

**Reset**:
A History Log event recording that the learner chose to start over. Schedules and progress are calculated only from events after the latest Reset; earlier events stay in the log but stop counting. It syncs like any other event, so one Reset starts every device over.
_Avoid_: Wipe, delete history, clear data

**Learned**:
An Item or Sentence whose introducing card (a letter card, or the Listening Card of the Sentence that introduces it) was graded Good the last time it was reviewed. Graded Again, it stops counting until passed again. Progress counts are built on this. A new Sentence uses a looser bar to decide whether its other Items are familiar enough: a word or grammar Item counts once its introducing Listening Card has been graded Good at least once (a later Again doesn't undo it), and a letter or sound counts once its letter card has been reviewed at all.
_Avoid_: Known, mastered, completed

**Study Session**:
Focused time at the PC for meeting new material through listening and reading at the learner's level, followed by Conversation Practice.
_Avoid_: Lesson, class

**Conversation Practice**:
Spoken back-and-forth with Claude during a Study Session. Claude corrects the learner's mistakes and they try again.
_Avoid_: Chat, tutoring, roleplay

**Commute Lesson**:
Audio-only study for driving: spoken recall drills plus stories at the learner's level.
_Avoid_: Podcast, audio lesson

**Record and Compare**:
Pronunciation practice where the learner hears their recording back to back with native audio, with both pitch curves shown.
_Avoid_: Pronunciation check, shadowing

## Evidence

**Method**:
A learning technique the platform uses, such as spaced repetition or retrieval practice. Every Method has an Evidence Tier and a citation.
_Avoid_: Technique, hack, strategy

**Evidence Tier**:
How strongly research backs a Method: _strong_, or _promising_ (used, but flagged as weaker). Methods without research support are excluded, so they have no tier.
_Avoid_: Confidence, rating
