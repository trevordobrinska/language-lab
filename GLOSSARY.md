# Language Lab

A personal platform for reaching conversational fluency in several languages, one after another, using only learning methods shown to work in research.

## Language

**Target Language**:
One of the languages being learned: Korean, Spanish, Mandarin, Japanese, Polish.
_Avoid_: Course, track, L2

**Active Language**:
The single Target Language currently receiving new study. There is exactly one at a time.
_Avoid_: Current course, focus language

**Maintained Language**:
A Target Language that is no longer Active but still gets short review so it doesn't fade.
_Avoid_: Finished language, archived language

**Fluency Goal**:
CEFR B1 in speaking and listening for a Target Language. Reading and writing are learned only as far as they support speaking.
_Avoid_: Fluent, proficiency, done

**Mission**:
A concrete real-world conversation the learner wants to manage in a Target Language, such as chatting with the shop owner at a Korean market.
_Avoid_: Goal, scenario, quest

**Checkpoint**:
A periodic speaking and listening test that scores the learner against the CEFR "can-do" descriptors, to track progress toward the Fluency Goal.
_Avoid_: Exam, test, assessment

## Study

**Review**:
Spaced recall of sentences already met. The learner recalls aloud, reveals the answer and grades themselves. It happens on the phone, anywhere.
_Avoid_: Flashcards, SRS, drill

**Study Session**:
Focused time at the PC for meeting new material through listening and reading at the learner's level, followed by Conversation Practice.
_Avoid_: Lesson, class

**Conversation Practice**:
Spoken back-and-forth with Claude during a Study Session. Claude corrects the learner's mistakes and they try again.
_Avoid_: Chat, tutoring, roleplay

**Commute Lesson**:
Audio-only study for driving: spoken recall drills plus stories at the learner's level.
_Avoid_: Podcast, audio lesson

**Record and Compare**:
Pronunciation practice where the learner hears their recording back to back with native audio, with both pitch curves shown.
_Avoid_: Pronunciation check, shadowing

## Evidence

**Method**:
A learning technique the platform uses, such as spaced repetition or retrieval practice. Every Method has an Evidence Tier and a citation.
_Avoid_: Technique, hack, strategy

**Evidence Tier**:
How strongly research backs a Method: _strong_, or _promising_ (used, but flagged as weaker). Methods without research support are excluded, so they have no tier.
_Avoid_: Confidence, rating
